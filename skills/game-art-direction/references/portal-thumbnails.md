# What winning portal thumbnails share (observed 24 Oct 2026, CrazyGames and Poki category pages)

Thumbnails are the shop window. What the winners share:
- Saturated, bright colours on a strong single background colour. Almost no dull or muted tiles; the few muted ones (text-only, plain screenshots) look broken next to the rest.
- A big chunky title in a heavy rounded display font with a thick dark outline and often a drop shadow, filling 30 to 50% of the tile. Readable at about 200 px wide (CrazyGames grid) and still recognisable at about 94 px (Poki small tiles).
- One hero subject (character, object, or the core mechanic: a giant pickaxe, a coin, a capybara), large and centred, often cut off by the frame for scale. Many are glossy 3D renders or soft-shaded 2D.
- The mechanic is legible from the tile: blocks for block puzzles, a hand-tap icon for clickers, jars of colour for sort puzzles.
- Poki tiles are square with rounded corners and often have no text at all, just one bold object (a duck on a trampoline, a watermelon). Poki's big featured tiles are 2x2.

Genres seen at the top (24 Oct, Lebanon IP, so lists are geo-tuned):
- CrazyGames clicker: Leek Factory Tycoon, The MachinEGG, Capybara Clicker (a capybara clicker already exists), Mr. Mine, Clicker Heroes, Idle Mining Empire, Planet Clicker, Babel Tower, Tiny Tribes. Many "tycoon" and "idle X" names.
- CrazyGames puzzle: merge games dominate (Piece of Cake: Merge and Bake, Designville, Mergest Kingdom), then sort and screw puzzles, mahjong, block puzzles.
- Poki: Blocky Blast Puzzle sits on the home page; clicker list led by Spacebar Clicker, Duck Merge, Kiwi Clicker, Idle Ants.
